I am a space weather PhD Student in the University of Reading's [Department of Meteorology](https://www.reading.ac.uk/meteorology/) supervised by Prof. Mathew Owens, Dr. Luke Barnard, and Dr. Matthew Lang. I am a Met Office CASE award student with Dr Mike Marsh, Dr Siegfried Gonzi, and Dr. Harriet Turner at the [UK Met Office](https://weather.metoffice.gov.uk/specialist-forecasts/space-weather). My main research interests are in heliospheric modelling, solar wind ensemble forecasting, and statistical uncertainty.
