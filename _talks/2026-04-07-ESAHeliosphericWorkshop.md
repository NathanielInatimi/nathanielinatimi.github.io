I attended ESA's Space Weather and Heliospheric Modelling Workshop as a representative of the Space and Atmospheric Electricity research group at Reading. I presented recent modelling developments in *"Computationally efficient, dynamical solar wind forecasting using in-situ solar wind observations"* from the SPATE research group at Reading's Meteorology Department.
