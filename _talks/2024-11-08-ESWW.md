I attended ESWW 2024 in Coimbra, Portugal where I presented in Session CD4 -- Open Validation of Space Weather Models Throughout the Heliosphere: Challenges and Future Directions. I presented initial results from my PhD which I have recently written up into a paper on *"Adapting Ensemble-Calibration Techniques to Probabilistic Solar-Wind Forecasting"* that has just been accepted by AGU Space Weather journal!
